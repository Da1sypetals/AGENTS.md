# doc

You are to create a document.

The document is meant to be read by an AI (LLM), **not** by human. Therefore:

DO NOT use emoji. DO NOT beautify your language. Do not brag or boast about your achievements.

DO NOT use ASCII art in your documentation. If (and only if) you need a diagram (you may not even need one, but in case), you must use mermaid. If you don't need one, do not create one.
